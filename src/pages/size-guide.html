How to compare

  1. Pick a favourite

    Choose something you own that fits the way you like.

  2. Lay it flat

    Button it up and smooth it out on a table or the floor.

  3. Measure the same way

    Use the points below, in centimetres, and compare with the piece's measurements.

How we measure

Every measurement is taken with the piece lying flat, unstretched.

A note on older sizing

The labelled size is shown on every piece, but sizing has changed over the years and between makers, so treat it as a guide. The measurements are the truth.
