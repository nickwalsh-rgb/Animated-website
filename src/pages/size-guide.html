How to measure

  1. Chest

    Around the fullest part, under the arms, tape level.

  2. Waist

    Around your natural waist, where your trousers sit.

  3. Sleeve

    From the centre back of your neck, over the shoulder, to the wrist.

  4. Between sizes?

    Take your usual size for a close fit, or size up for room to layer.
