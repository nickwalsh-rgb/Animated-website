What we aim for

We build this site to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What that means in practice

  • Everything can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Pages are structured with headings, landmarks and labels for screen readers.
  • Text and controls meet contrast requirements, and text can be resized to 200% without loss.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives.
  • Moving content can be paused, and motion is reduced when your device asks for it.
  • Forms have visible labels and say clearly what went wrong.
