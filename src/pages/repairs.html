What we mend

  • Seams and hems that have come undone
  • Lost buttons, broken zips and press studs
  • Holes and worn elbows in knitwear
  • Torn pockets and linings
  • Rewaxing for waxed cotton

Clothes are made to be worn, and wear shows. We'll always tell you honestly if something is past mending.

How it works

  1. Tell us what's wrong

    Email {{biz:email}} with a photo and, if you have it, your order number.

  2. Send it in

    We'll reply with where to send it. Please wash or air it first.

  3. We mend it

    We'll confirm the repair with you before we start, then do the work.

  4. We send it back

    Mended, pressed and ready for the next few years.

Do it yourself

Some repairs are quicker at home. The journal has guides to darning a small hole and rewaxing a jacket.

Faulty, not worn?

If something arrives faulty or fails sooner than it should, that's not a repair, it's on us. Tell us and we'll put it right. This doesn't affect your legal rights.
