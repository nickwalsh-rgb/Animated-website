Questions

How long do I have?
30 days from the day your order is delivered.
Can I exchange for another size or colour?
Yes, if the size or colour you want is still in stock. Tell us what you'd like instead when you book the return and we'll hold it for you. Exchanges are free.
How long does a refund take?
We check every return within 3 working days of it reaching us. The refund then goes back to your original payment method; your bank may take a few more days to show it.
Are gift cards returnable?
Gift cards can't be returned once sent, but you can still cancel within 14 days of buying one if it hasn't been used.
Something wrong with your order?
If anything arrives faulty or not as described, tell us and we'll put it right or refund you, including postage. This doesn't affect your legal rights.
