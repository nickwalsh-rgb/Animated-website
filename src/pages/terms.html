Your right to cancel

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of receiving it. Tell us within that time, then return the piece within 14 days of telling us. We'll refund the price and the standard delivery cost within 14 days of getting the piece back.

On top of that, our own 30-day returns give you longer for most pieces. Pieces marked "Final sale" are excluded from the 30-day returns, but your 14-day right to cancel still applies.

If something is wrong

Your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 are not affected. If a piece is faulty or not as described, contact us and we'll put it right or refund you, including return postage.
