What we do

We don't make clothes for a season. We find them, keep them and pass them on. Every piece in the archive is chosen by hand, repaired where it needs it, stitched with our label and numbered, then released once. When it's gone, it's gone.

How a piece gets here

  1. Found

    Sourced one at a time, never by the bale.

  2. Repaired

    Cleaned, mended and checked before it's listed. Any wear we leave is described and photographed.

  3. Numbered

    Stitched with the Archive 88 woven label and given its own number, so it stays on the record.

  4. Released

    Released in a drop on a set date. One of each, no restocks.

Who we are

Archive 88 is a small archive of clothes that were made to last and still have years left in them.

It started with the pieces that never wore out. A chore coat softened by someone else's winters. A jumper that had already been mended once and was better for it. Jeans faded into the shape of a life. Clothes like that deserve another owner, not a landfill.

So we look for them, one at a time. We clean them, mend what needs mending, stitch in our label and give each one its number. Then we let it go, once.

Most of what we find was made for being outside: the coast, the woods, the long way round. That's where we photograph it, and where we hope you'll wear it.
