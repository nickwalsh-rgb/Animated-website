What we do

We don't make clothes for a season. We find them, keep them and pass them on. Every piece in the archive is chosen by hand, repaired where it needs it, stitched with our label and numbered, then released once. When it's gone, it's gone.

How a piece gets here

  1. Found

    Sourced one at a time, never by the bale.

  2. Repaired

    Cleaned, mended and checked before it's listed. Any wear we leave is described and photographed.

  3. Numbered

    Stitched with the Archive 88 woven label and given its own number, so it stays on the record.

  4. Released

    Released in a drop on a set date. One of each, no restocks.
