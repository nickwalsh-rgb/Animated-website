What we make

Archive 88 is a small collection, chosen one piece at a time. Coats, knitwear, shirts, trousers, jersey and tailoring in wool, cotton, linen and wax. Each style is made in a limited run and given its own number, so you always know exactly which piece you own.

How we work

  1. Chosen, not churned

    A piece only joins the archive when it earns its place. We would rather make fewer things well.

  2. Limited runs

    Each style is made in a small run. When a size is gone it may not come back, so the stock you see is the stock there is.

  3. Natural fibres

    Cloth chosen to soften and wear in, not wear out.

  4. Repairs for life

    If something gives, send it back and we will mend it.

Who we are

Archive 88 started with the clothes that never wore out: a chore coat softened by someone else's winters, a jumper that had been mended once and was better for it. We wanted to find and make clothes like that.

Most of what we make is meant for being outside: the coast, the woods, the long way round. That is where the pine and the ridge in our mark come from, and where we hope you will wear it.
