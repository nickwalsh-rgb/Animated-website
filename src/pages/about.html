What we make

Archive 88 makes one collection and keeps it. Coats, knitwear, shirts, trousers, jersey and tailoring in wool, cotton, linen and wax, cut in fits that stay in the range season after season. No drops to chase and no sell-by dates: the coat you buy this winter is the one we'll still make next year.

How we work

  1. Natural fibres

    Cloth from mills that have spun for generations, chosen to soften and wear in, not wear out.

  2. Kept in every size

    Every style stays in the range, in every size, so you can come back for the same thing.

  3. Honest construction

    Quiet details, strong seams and nothing added that will date it.

  4. Repairs for life

    If something gives, send it back and we'll mend it.

Who we are

Archive 88 started with the clothes that never wore out: a chore coat softened by someone else's winters, a jumper that had been mended once and was better for it. We wanted to make clothes like that, and then keep making them.

Most of what we make is meant for being outside: the coast, the woods, the long way round. That's where we photograph it, and where we hope you'll wear it.
