Wool and cashmere

Air after wearing and wash only when it needs it: by hand in cool water with wool detergent, or on a machine wool cycle if the label allows. Dry flat on a towel, away from heat. Store folded.

How to wash wool, and how rarely

Cotton shirts

Wash at 30 or 40°C with similar colours, collars unbuttoned. Reshape while damp and hang to dry. Iron slightly damp if you want a crisp finish.

Jersey: tees and sweatshirts

Wash inside out at 30°C. Line dry or dry flat: tumble drying is what shrinks jersey and wears out the ribbing.

Linen

Wash at 30 or 40°C, without fabric softener. Shake out and hang to dry. It will crease, and it's meant to.

Linen creases. That's the point.

Waxed cotton

Never machine wash, dry clean or use hot water or soap. Brush off dry dirt and sponge with cold water. Rewax when rain stops beading on the surface.

Rewaxing a jacket at home

Denim

Wash rarely, inside out, in cold water, on its own for the first few washes: dark indigo will transfer. Hang to dry.

Tailoring and coats

Brush after wearing and hang on a shaped hanger so the shoulders keep their line. Dry clean only when needed; steam takes care of most creases.
