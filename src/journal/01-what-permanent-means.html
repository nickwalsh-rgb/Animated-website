Most clothing is made to be replaced. A style arrives, sells through and is gone by the time you've worked out that you like it. We make one collection and keep it.

The same thing, next year

Every style in the collection stays in the range. The coat you buy this winter is the one we'll still make next winter, in the same cloth and the same cut. If you wear through the elbows of a jumper in five years, you can come back for the same jumper.

It also means nothing here is on a clock. There are no drops to chase and no end-of-season rush, so you can take your time and buy the thing once.

Every size, kept in stock

A permanent style isn't much use if your size sold out in the first week. We hold every style in every size, and when one runs low we make more of it. Once you know your size in a fit, it stays your size.

What changes, and what doesn't

We do change things, slowly. A pocket that didn't sit right gets moved. A colour is added when a mill offers a cloth worth having. What we don't do is retire something that works to make room for something new.

The measure we use is simple: would we still want to wear it in ten years? If the answer is yes, it stays.
