# Archive 88

Scroll-animated shop front for **Archive 88**, built from the Figma brand file ("05 Quiet Caps" wordmark,
A88 monogram, woven label). Ready to fill with real pieces, photos and drop dates, and to move to Shopify.

Plain HTML, CSS and JS. GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis are vendored in `assets/vendor/`, fonts are self-hosted
in `assets/fonts/`. Pages are generated by a small Python script (standard library only).

## Editing on GitHub (no setup needed)

You can edit `data/products.json`, `data/site.json`, the pages in `src/` or upload photos straight on github.com.
When the change lands on `main`, the **Build site** workflow (`.github/workflows/build.yml`) rebuilds the pages,
makes web-sized photos, commits the result and publishes it. It also runs **every hour**, so a drop goes live
automatically within the hour of its date. You can run it any time from the **Actions** tab ("Run workflow").

## Everyday editing

| To change | Edit | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pieces (name, price, size, measurements, condition, sold) | `data/products.json` | `python3 build.py` |
| Product photos | add files to `assets/products/<handle>/` (see that folder's README) | `python3 build.py` |
| Drops (names and dates), social links, business details, shipping text, returns FAQ, categories | `data/site.json` | `python3 build.py` |
| About, Contact, Size guide, Privacy, Terms | `src/pages/*.html` | `python3 build.py` |
| Shop the look (looks, markers, photos) | `data/lookbook.json` + photos in `assets/lookbook/<handle>.jpg` | `python3 build.py` |
| Page layout and copy | `src/partials/*.html` | `python3 build.py` |
| Styles / motion | `assets/css/style.css`, `assets/js/*.js` | refresh |

`index.html`, `shop/`, `record/`, `products/`, the info page folders (`about/`, `contact/`...), `shopify/products.csv`,
`sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` are **generated**. Don't edit them by hand.

### Drops

`drops.list` in `data/site.json` holds every drop with its date and time (UK). Each piece has a `drop`.
- The homepage shows the **latest drop whose date has passed**.
- Pieces in a **future** drop are kept off the site until its date (they're already in the Shopify CSV).
- The next future drop drives the **countdown** in the Join section and the date in the hero.

### Photos

Put photos in `assets/products/<handle>/` (`01.jpg`, `02.jpg`...). The build (with Pillow installed, as it is on GitHub)
makes WebP copies at 480, 960 and 1600 px plus a 1200 px JPEG in a `web/` folder, and pages use the right size for each screen.

```bash
python3 build.py              # rebuild everything
python3 -m http.server 8080   # preview at http://localhost:8080
```

The build stops with a clear message if a product is missing a field, has a duplicate number or an unknown
category, and lists any pieces that are still waiting for photos.

## Pages

**Homepage** `index.html`
- Hero: wordmark, one line, the next drop date and "Shop the archive". The 3-second loader plays on the first visit of a session only.
- Film: a small window opens into the graded forest film.
- Story, then **the archive**: a pinned sideways row of hang tags with a thin progress line. Each tag shows the first photo (or the monogram until photos exist), links to its product page, and has Add to bag. Sold pieces stay, greyed out with a Sold stamp.
- Label, marquee, **Returns** and **Join the list** (sharing one colour band; the email field has a visible label).
- **Chapter rail**: once the first chapter scrolls in, a slim index on the right (Story, Archive, Label, Look, Returns, Join) shows
  where you are and jumps to any chapter. Built from `data-chapter` on each section. On phones it becomes a thin progress line at the top.
- **Join the list**: checked when you leave the field, then Joining / Joined / error states. The real sign-up goes in
  `window.A88.subscribe(email)` (return a promise); until then it succeeds locally.

**Product pages** `products/<handle>/`
- Gallery of every photo in the piece's folder. With no photos yet it shows labelled slots from the shot list (Front, Back, Label, Detail, Flaws). Phones swipe sideways with a pager line.
- Number, name, price, size / era / condition, description, Add to bag (or Sold + Join the list), final-sale note when set.
- Details: measurements table (cm), condition and flaws, material, shipping, returns.
- **Check the fit**: enter one of your own measurements; it says how this piece compares (and shows it on a small scale).
  Your numbers are remembered on this device per measurement, so they carry over to other pieces.
- **Zoom**: with real photos, a zoom button (or clicking the photo) opens a full-screen viewer: click to magnify at that spot, move to look around, arrows for the next photo.
- **Phones**: a slim Add to bag bar pins to the bottom whenever the main button is off screen (Join the list for sold pieces).
- More from the archive: three related pieces.
- Own share preview (product photo when available), `product:*` Open Graph tags and Product + Breadcrumb structured data for Google.

**Shop all** `shop/`: every available piece in a grid. Filter by category and size, include sold pieces; filters are kept in the
address (`shop/?cat=knitwear&size=l`) so the menu and breadcrumbs link straight to a filtered view. Tags glide into place (GSAP Flip).

**Shop the look** `lookbook/` (first look also featured on the homepage): editorial photos with numbered markers on each
garment. Hover (mouse) or tap (touch) a marker to open a card with the piece, its price, **Add to bag** and a link to it; click pins
the card, Esc or clicking elsewhere closes it, and on phones the card docks on the side of the photo away from the marker.
The list beside each photo mirrors the markers. Looks, markers (x/y in % of the photo) and captions live in `data/lookbook.json`;
until a photo exists a labelled slot is shown. Pieces from future drops are left out automatically.
**Add the whole look** (shown when two or more pieces are available) adds every piece not already in the bag.

**The record** `record/`: every piece ever released, grouped by drop, with sold pieces kept on the list.

**Info pages:** About, Contact, Size guide, Privacy, Terms of sale. Business details come from `data/site.json`; anything missing
shows a red "To be confirmed" marker, and Privacy/Terms show a "Draft" note until `legal_reviewed` is set to `true`.

**Shared on every page:** category menu (counts come from the data; on product pages a category goes to that piece on the homepage), bag drawer saved in the browser (with Undo after removing a piece, and a few available pieces when it's empty), raised social keys, footer.
Pages cross-fade into each other, and a piece's hang-tag photo morphs into its product photo (View Transitions, in browsers that support them).
Back returns you to where you were on the page. Skip link, current-page marking, 44px tap targets and screen-reader announcements for the bag throughout.

## Motion

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| Loader (first visit) | Letters rise while a counter runs 00 to 88, then the panel lifts |
| Hero | Letters rise in, then drift apart at different speeds as you scroll |
| Film | Window opens to full screen; caption rises in |
| Story | Words light up as you read |
| Archive | Pinned sideways scroll; tags swing with scroll speed |
| Product page | First photo unmasks upward, details rise in; later photos unmask on scroll |
| Menu / bag / accordions | Wipe, slide and measured-height animations |
| Social keys | Lift and tilt toward the cursor; press flush on click |
| Every button | Sinks on press and springs back on release (mouse, touch and keyboard); primary buttons ripple from the point pressed |
| Shop the look | Markers pop in one by one; cards open from their marker; the matching list row lights up |
| Page to page | Cross-fade; hang tag morphs into the product photo |
| Chapter rail | Active tick stretches; labels slide in on hover |
| Bag | Whole look adds piece by piece; Undo toast rises in; a short vibration on add (Android) |

Timings come from shared tokens: `--t-fast` / `--t-base` / `--t-slow` in CSS and `A88.T` in JS.

`prefers-reduced-motion` turns all of it off. Revealed content fades with opacity only, so keyboard users can reach it before it scrolls into view.

## Brand

| Token | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Ink | `#1B211D` |
| Bone | `#E6E3DA` |
| Pine | `#2F3D33` |
| Paper | `#F5F4F0` |
| Accent: burnt orange | `#A3471F` (hover `#8A3A18`) |

**Accent rules:** light sections only (paper, bone); it's too dark to read on ink or pine.
It marks *signals* (bag count, next drop date, countdown, archive progress, lookbook markers, Sold stamps,
focus rings, text selection) and **one primary action per page**: "Shop the archive" on the homepage, "Add to bag"
on product pages and lookbook cards, "Checkout" in the bag. Every other button stays ink. Errors and placeholders
use ink (dashed outline / "!"), never the accent, so orange always means "look here".

Type: **Archivo Narrow** (wordmark, display) and **Archivo** (body), self-hosted. Instagram/Facebook icons are from Phosphor Icons (MIT).
The film (`assets/video/forest.mp4`) is the supplied clip, graded warm and muted, crossfade-looped, no audio.

## Before launch

All product data is **sample data**: names, prices, sizes, measurements, materials and conditions in `data/products.json`.

- [ ] Real pieces in `data/products.json`, photos in `assets/products/<handle>/`
- [ ] Real drops and dates in `data/site.json` (samples: Season one 2 October 2026, Season two 4 December 2026, 7pm)
- [ ] Business details in `data/site.json`: currently **placeholders** (fictional company, Ofcom drama phone number, `.example` email)
- [ ] The About story in `src/pages/about.html` is a **draft** written from the site's look: edit it to your real story
- [ ] The providers list in `src/pages/privacy.html`
- [ ] Lookbook photos in `assets/lookbook/` and marker positions in `data/lookbook.json` (looks are placeholders)
- [ ] Privacy and Terms reviewed, then set `"legal_reviewed": true` in `data/site.json`
- [ ] Real Instagram/Facebook URLs in `data/site.json`
- [ ] Real shipping wording in `data/site.json`
- [ ] Returns wording checked against your policy and UK consumer law (14-day right to cancel online orders)
- [ ] Site URL in `data/site.json` if not using `nickwalsh-rgb.github.io/Animated-website`
- [ ] Shopify set up: see [docs/SHOPIFY.md](docs/SHOPIFY.md). Checkout and email sign-up are wired there.
