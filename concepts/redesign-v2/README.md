# Archive 88: homepage overhaul (concept, not deployed)

Rebuilt to the taste-skill's system (Leonxlnx/taste-skill 2.5.1). Open `index.html` over a local server
(`python3 -m http.server` in this folder): its scripts are ES modules and do not run from `file://`.

- Kind: product (natural-fibre clothing sold online). Gate: PASS, one waiver (`.taste-skill/waivers.json`).
- Deal: hero film (the client's own forest film), nav-mega, close-word, footer-columns, dark-band, pill buttons.
  Palette: the brand's own colours. Type: Bricolage Grotesque and Figtree. Moments: word-highlight, photo-chapters.

## Stand-ins to replace with Archive 88's own photographs
Every garment, cloth and repair photograph is a Wikimedia Commons stand-in (credits.html):
- Outerwear, Knitwear, Shirts tiles: the range's own garments on one plain backdrop, one angle.
- The four cloths: close-ups of the Donegal tweed, cotton chambray, selvedge denim and Geelong lambswool used.
- Repairs: Archive 88's own repairs (darning, re-knitting a cuff, a seam on the machine).
- Delivery: the rail or packing table.
- The film: a higher-resolution master (the 1280x720 file is enlarged on tall tablets).
